# Pricing Validation Research Design

## 1. Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter
Ask users the following 4 questions:
1. At what price would you consider BiasGuard to be so expensive that you would not consider buying it? (Too Expensive)
2. At what price would you consider BiasGuard to be priced so low that you would feel the quality couldn't be very good? (Too Cheap)
3. At what price would you consider BiasGuard starting to get expensive, so that it is not out of the question, but you would have to give some thought to buying it? (High/Premium)
4. At what price would you consider BiasGuard to be a bargain—a great buy for the money? (Cheap/Good Value)

## 2. Subscription vs. One-Time Payment Preference
* "Would you prefer to pay a monthly subscription for continuous insights, or a one-time fee for a lifetime license with limited future updates?"
* Measure the split in preference.

## 3. Willingness-To-Pay (WTP) Scenarios
Test conversion intent at the following specific price points:
* **$1.99 / month:** "Would you subscribe today at this price?" (Yes/No)
* **$3.99 / month:** "Would you subscribe today at this price?" (Yes/No)
* **$6.99 / month:** "Would you subscribe today at this price?" (Yes/No)
* **Annual Plan ($39.99/yr):** Test upfront commitment.
* **Lifetime Deal ($149):** Test upfront capital capture.

## 4. Feature Ranking Exercise (Forced Ranking)
Ask users to rank these 14 features from 1 (Most Valuable) to 14 (Least Valuable):
1. Disposition Effect Analysis
2. FOMO Trade Detection
3. Overconfidence Tracking
4. Revenge Trading Alerts
5. Manual Trade Entry UI
6. Broker Sync (Plaid/Yodlee)
7. CSV Import
8. Personal Trading Journal
9. Peer Benchmarking (Compare to others)
10. Weekly Email Reports
11. Push Notification Alerts
12. Export to PDF/Excel
13. Educational Content/Library
14. Dark Mode

## 5. MaxDiff-Like Exercise
Present sets of 4 features at a time (from the list above) and ask users to select:
* The **MOST** valuable feature in the set.
* The **LEAST** valuable feature in the set.
(Repeat across multiple sets to calculate relative utility scores for all features).
