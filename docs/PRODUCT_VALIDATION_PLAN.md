# Product Validation Plan (Final Report)

1. **Biggest unknown**: Will users consistently input their trades manually over a long period?
2. **Riskiest assumption**: That awareness of cognitive bias actually changes user behavior enough for them to perceive value.
3. **Primary user**: Intermediate retail traders (crypto or stocks) who have blown an account and realize psychology is their weakness.
4. **Primary job**: Act as an objective, emotionless mirror to stop bad trading decisions in the moment.
5. **Why care**: It saves them real money by preventing catastrophic emotional losses.
6. **Why not care**: It introduces friction into an already stressful activity; they might prefer denial.
7. **Manual-entry hypothesis**: Users will tolerate manual entry if the insights generated are immediately actionable and highly accurate.
8. **Cold-start**: Provide users with a "Quick Assessment" quiz during onboarding to generate an initial psychological profile before any trades are logged.
9. **Pricing**: $9.99/month or $79.99/year. Must be cheaper than a blown trade, but high enough to filter for serious users.
10. **Sub hypothesis**: Users who experience a "cooldown period" prompted by the app will retain 3x longer than those who just view stats.
11. **Retention**: Weekly check-ins, push notifications summarizing the week's emotional performance vs financial performance.
12. **Research plan**: Conduct 10-20 user interviews over the next two weeks using the sample structure defined in our analytics plan.
13. **Interview plan**: Focus on past failures. "Tell me about the last time you broke your trading rules. What were you feeling?"
14. **Feature ranking plan**: Use a forced-ranking survey (Kano Model) to determine if users value API auto-sync over deeper AI insights.
15. **Judge sim summary**: The pitch must pivot from "cool AI tech" to "behavioral finance tool that makes/saves money." Address the manual entry friction immediately.
16. **Demo risks**: Network failure during AI generation, and boring data entry sequences.
17. **Metrics to measure**: Activation (First Risk Review), North Star (Weekly Active Risk Reviewers), Day 1 / Day 7 Retention.
18. **Post-launch exp**: Run an A/B test on the onboarding flow (Quiz first vs. Log Trade first).
19. **What not to build yet**: Exchange API auto-sync. It's too complex and risky before validating the core journaling loop.

MOST IMPORTANT UNVALIDATED ASSUMPTION: Users will consistently manually log their trades when emotional.
FIRST USER RESEARCH QUESTION TO ANSWER: How currently do you track your trading psychology and mistakes?
FEATURE FREEZE SHOULD REMAIN: YES
