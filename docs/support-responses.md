# Customer Support Response Templates

## Login failed
Hello,
We're sorry to hear you're having trouble logging in. Please ensure you have a stable internet connection and that you are using the correct login method (Google, Apple, or Email). If the problem persists, please try resetting your password or reinstalling the app. Let us know if you need further assistance.

## Portfolio missing
Hello,
If your portfolio appears empty or missing, please first check that you are logged into the correct account. Also, ensure your device is connected to the internet so the app can sync with our servers. If your data is still missing, please let us know so we can investigate further.

## Market data unavailable
Hello,
We apologize for the inconvenience. Market data may occasionally be unavailable due to server maintenance or issues with our data provider. Please note that we use End-of-Day (EOD) data, so real-time prices are not supported. If the data does not refresh after a few hours, please check your internet connection or reach out again.

## Risk metric unavailable
Hello,
Risk metrics may be displayed as N/A or unavailable if there is insufficient historical data for the assets in your portfolio. As more data becomes available, these metrics will automatically update. 

## Purchase completed but Pro not unlocked
Hello,
Thank you for your purchase. If BiasGuard Pro is not unlocked yet, please go to the Settings screen in the app and tap "Restore Purchases." This should sync your App Store/Play Store receipt with our system. If the issue remains, please provide us with your purchase receipt.

## Restore failed
Hello,
We're sorry the restore process failed. Please ensure you are logged into your device with the same Apple ID or Google Play account used to make the original purchase. Also, check your internet connection and try again.

## Cancel subscription
Hello,
To cancel your BiasGuard Pro subscription, you must do so directly through your device's app store. 
- iOS: Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions.
- Android: Go to Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions.
Please note that deleting the app or your BiasGuard account will not cancel your subscription.

## Delete Account
Hello,
You can permanently delete your BiasGuard account and all associated data directly from the app. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Please be aware that deleting your account will not cancel any active subscriptions.

## Behavioral result question
Hello,
Thank you for reaching out. The behavioral insights provided by BiasGuard are educational heuristics designed to help you understand potential investment biases. They are not professional psychological diagnoses. If you have specific questions about your results, please feel free to ask!

## Privacy request
Hello,
We take your privacy seriously. If you would like to request a copy of your data or have it permanently deleted from our servers, you can initiate account deletion within the app (Settings > Account > Delete Account). For further inquiries regarding our privacy practices, please let us know.
