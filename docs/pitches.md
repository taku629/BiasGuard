# BiasGuard Pitches

## 30-Second Elevator Pitch
"Retail investors constantly monitor the market, but rarely monitor themselves. BiasGuard is an analytics platform that helps investors see both the risk in their portfolio and the patterns in their own decisions. By analyzing your transaction history and utilizing interactive assessments, we identify behavioral blind spots like overconfidence and the disposition effect. It's not about what to buy or sell—it's about making you a more self-aware, resilient investor."

## 60-Second Demo Pitch
"Welcome to BiasGuard. Most tools focus on the market, but we focus on the investor. The core message is simple: see the risk in your portfolio and the patterns in your own decisions. 
When you connect your data, our Risk Dashboard instantly calculates the historical risk profile of your current composition—Metrics like Sharpe Ratio, Max Drawdown, and Beta.
Then, we move to the Behavioral Radar, mapping your trading history against 5 key behavioral signals. For example, if you hold losing positions too long or anchor to reference prices, BiasGuard alerts you. Every insight is fully explainable—no black boxes, no guaranteed returns, just objective transparency into your own habits. We also offer a Pro tier via RevenueCat for advanced analytics. BiasGuard: Know your portfolio, know yourself."
