# User Interview Script (15-20 Min)

*Note: Do NOT explain BiasGuard or mention its name at the beginning of the interview.*

## 1. Problem Interview (5 mins)
* Tell me about the last time you made an investment decision you later regretted.
* What was your current behavior/process leading up to that trade?
* How frequently do you feel emotions dictate your trading?
* What do you think is the financial cost of these emotional decisions?
* How do you currently try to prevent this? (Current solutions)

## 2. Solution Interview (5 mins)
*(Briefly describe the concept: "A tool that analyzes your past trades to show you your hidden behavioral biases.")*
* What is your initial comprehension of this idea? What does it do in your own words?
* What is the perceived value of knowing this information?
* Would you trust an app to analyze your decision-making? Why or why not?

## 3. Manual Entry Experiment (3 mins)
* If you were to use this tool, how would you prefer to get your data in?
  * **Option A:** Manually entering each trade.
  * **Option B:** Uploading a CSV from your broker.
  * **Option C:** Direct broker sync (Plaid/etc).
* *Probe:* What are the trade-offs for you between these options? (Note: Do not assume privacy is the advantage for manual—let them state their reasons).

## 4. Cold Start Test (2 mins)
*(Show them the first screen post-signup with no data).*
* Imagine you just downloaded this app and opened it. What would you do in the first 5 minutes?
* How does this empty screen make you feel? (Overwhelmed, confused, clear?)

## 5. 5-Second Test (2 mins)
*(Flash the main dashboard UI for 5 seconds, then hide it).*
* What do you remember seeing?
* What do you think you can do on that page?
* What stood out the most?

## 6. One-Liner Test (2 mins)
Which of these descriptions makes you want to use the app the most?
* **A:** "A trading journal that makes you a better investor."
* **B:** "Identify the behavioral biases costing you money in the market."
* **C:** "Stop emotional trading with data-driven insights."
* **D:** "Your personal AI trading psychologist."
* **E (Recommended):** "Protect your portfolio from your own worst enemy: you."

## 7. Landing Page Message Test (1 min)
Which headline resonates most?
1. **Variation 1:** "Trade smarter, not harder."
2. **Variation 2:** "See the blind spots in your investing strategy."
3. **Variation 3:** "Quantify your emotions and boost your returns."
