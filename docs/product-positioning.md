# Product Positioning

**One-liner:** "BiasGuard helps investors see both portfolio risk and patterns in their own decisions."

## Target User
Retail investors who manage their own portfolios, have some experience in the market, and are looking for advanced analytics to improve their decision-making process. They are self-directed but recognize they might have blind spots.

## Core Problem
Retail investors obsess over market movements but lack insight into their own behavioral biases (like holding losers too long or trading too frequently) and the true risk exposure of their aggregated portfolio. 

## Core Benefit
BiasGuard provides objective clarity. It quantifies both the statistical risk of current holdings and the behavioral tendencies of the investor, turning abstract psychological concepts into actionable, explainable metrics.

## Differentiator
Unlike standard portfolio trackers that only show P&L and asset allocation, BiasGuard acts as an analytical and behavioral layer. It focuses on *how* you invest, not just *what* you hold, using evidence-based signals and assessments.

## Why Now
The democratization of trading (zero-commission brokers, fractional shares) has led to an explosion of self-directed investors. In volatile markets, managing one's own psychology and understanding true portfolio risk is more critical than ever. 

## Why BiasGuard vs Normal Trackers
Normal trackers tell you your net worth and daily change. BiasGuard tells you your Sharpe Ratio, your Beta, and warns you if your trading patterns show signs of overconfidence or the disposition effect. It's a mirror for the investor, not just a window into the market.
