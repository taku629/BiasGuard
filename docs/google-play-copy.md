## Full Description (Max 4000 chars)
Most retail investors focus entirely on what the market is doing. But the biggest variable in long-term investing isn't the market—it's you. 

BiasGuard is a powerful analytics layer for your portfolio. We help you see both the objective risk in your current holdings and the behavioral patterns in your trading decisions. 

**PORTFOLIO RISK DASHBOARD**
Understand your exposure. BiasGuard calculates the historical risk profile of your current composition, giving you clear metrics:
• Volatility
• Sharpe Ratio
• Maximum Drawdown
• Beta (vs. S&P 500)

**BEHAVIORAL RADAR**
Discover your blind spots. By analyzing your transaction history and utilizing interactive assessments, BiasGuard scores you across 5 key behavioral signals:
• Disposition Effect (Holding losers too long)
• Loss Realization
• Overconfidence Calibration
• Information Selection
• Reference-Price Sensitivity (Anchoring)

**TOTAL TRANSPARENCY**
No black boxes. Every insight comes with complete explainability, so you know exactly why a signal was triggered based on your own data.

*Disclaimer: BiasGuard is an analytics tool. It does not provide guaranteed returns, attempts to beat the market, advice on what to buy or sell, or psychological diagnosis.*
