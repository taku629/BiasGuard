# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is it investment advice?**
A: No. BiasGuard provides educational risk analysis and behavioral insights based on historical data. It does not provide personalized investment, financial, or tax advice.

**Q: Why is Sharpe N/A?**
A: The Sharpe Ratio requires sufficient historical return data and a valid risk-free rate. If your portfolio has too little history or calculated volatility is zero, the Sharpe Ratio is displayed as N/A.

**Q: Why Behavioral Signal unavailable?**
A: Behavioral signals require completion of the psychological assessment. If you have not completed the assessment or if the data cannot be processed (e.g., offline or server error), the signal will be unavailable.

**Q: What is "needs more data"?**
A: This indicates that the asset or portfolio lacks sufficient historical market data to calculate meaningful risk metrics or behavioral insights.

**Q: Does it diagnose biases?**
A: No. The behavioral assessment is an educational tool designed to highlight potential behavioral tendencies (like loss aversion or overconfidence) based on heuristics. It is not a clinical or psychological diagnosis.

**Q: How is Pro determined?**
A: Pro status is determined by an active subscription managed through your app store (Apple App Store or Google Play Store) via RevenueCat.

**Q: How do I restore purchases?**
A: You can restore purchases by going to the Settings screen and tapping the "Restore Purchases" button. This will sync your past purchases with your current device.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Subscriptions must be canceled through your device's app store settings (Apple ID for iOS or Google Play for Android). You can find a link to "Manage Subscription" in the Settings screen.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: You can delete your account by going to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This will permanently remove your data from our servers.

**Q: Does account deletion cancel my subscription?**
A: No. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription. You must still cancel the subscription through your device's app store settings to prevent future charges.

**Q: Why is market data delayed?**
A: BiasGuard uses End-of-Day (EOD) market data. Prices and risk metrics reflect the close of the previous trading day and are not real-time.

**Q: What happens if I go offline?**
A: You can view previously loaded portfolio data and cached assessments. However, adding new assets, fetching updated risk metrics, completing new assessments, or purchasing subscriptions requires an active internet connection.
