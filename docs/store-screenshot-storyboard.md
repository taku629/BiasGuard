# App Store / Google Play Screenshot Storyboard

**Core Message:** "See the risk in your portfolio and the patterns in your own decisions."

## Screenshot 1: Home Dashboard
*   **Headline:** See Your Complete Portfolio
*   **Subtitle:** At-a-glance overview of your investments.
*   **UI Elements:** Total portfolio value, daily change, high-level asset allocation pie chart.
*   **Prerequisite Data:** Synced portfolio transactions and current market prices.
*   **Precautions (Privacy):** Anonymized mock data; no real PII or account numbers visible.
*   **Marketing Copy:** "Understand Your Portfolio Risk"

## Screenshot 2: Portfolio Risk
*   **Headline:** Deep Risk Analytics
*   **Subtitle:** Measure the real volatility of your holdings.
*   **UI Elements:** Volatility, Sharpe Ratio, Beta (vs SPY), and Maximum Drawdown metrics.
*   **Prerequisite Data:** Calculated risk metrics based on historical asset returns of the current composition.
*   **Precautions (Privacy):** Use standard ticker symbols with mock quantities.
*   **Marketing Copy:** "Objective Risk Metrics"
