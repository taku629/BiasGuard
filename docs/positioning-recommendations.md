# Positioning & Copy Recommendations

Based on the Competitive Intelligence Audit, the following recommendations are made for BiasGuard's store copy and marketing materials.

## 1. Avoid Generic Absolute Claims
**Current/Generic phrases to avoid:**
- *"The missing layer in investing"*
- *"The first/only app to track behavioral biases"*
- *"Unlike other portfolio trackers"*
- *"No other app combines risk and behavior"*

**Why:** Competitors like *FinanSee* and *Investor's Mirror* already exist in the behavioral space. Tools like *Nitrogen* do risk profiling. Stating "the only app" is factually unsafe and could cause Store rejection or user distrust. 

**Recommended Alternatives:**
- *"A new way to see your portfolio."*
- *"Bridging the gap between quantitative risk and behavioral decision-making."*
- *"See what standard trackers miss: your own behavior."*

## 2. Refine the Core One-Liner
**Generic:** "BiasGuard tracks your portfolio and shows your biases."
**Better:** "See the risk in your portfolio and the patterns in your own decisions."

**Why:** It emphasizes self-reflection and hard data (risk) without sounding diagnostic.

## 3. Emphasize "Transaction-Derived" Evidence
Many behavioral competitors rely purely on self-reported quizzes or journals. BiasGuard's edge is deriving metrics from actual trades.

**Recommended Addition to Store Description:**
*"BiasGuard doesn't just ask how you feel—it analyzes what you actually did. Our Disposition Effect and Loss Realization indicators are calculated directly from your transaction history."*

## 4. Frame Limitations as "Privacy & Intentionality"
Since BiasGuard lacks Broker Sync (a massive weakness compared to Delta/Sharesight), it should be framed as a deliberate privacy and mindfulness choice.

**Recommended Messaging:**
*"Manual transaction entry ensures complete privacy and forces you to mindfully review your trades. No creepy broker-syncing, no selling your financial data. Just you, your portfolio, and your analytics."*

## 5. Paywall / Value Differentiation
Instead of just saying "Upgrade to Pro," emphasize the shift from "monitoring" to "deep understanding."

**Recommended Pro Upsell Copy:**
*"BiasGuard Pro: Unlock multiple portfolios, the complete 5-Signal Behavioral Dashboard, and full formula explainability."*

## 6. Disclaimers Must Stand Out
Ensure it is exceedingly clear that BiasGuard is not a robo-advisor or diagnostic tool to avoid regulatory and competitive confusion.

**Recommended App Store Footer:**
*"BiasGuard is an educational analytics tool. It does not provide financial, investment, or psychological advice. Behavioral signals are generated via mathematical heuristics, not clinical diagnoses."*
