## 5 Riskiest Assumptions

1. **Manual Input**: Users are willing to manually input trade data. (Unvalidated)
2. **Willingness to Pay (WTP)**: Users will pay for a behavioral finance tool. (Unvalidated)
3. **Recurring Value**: The app provides recurring value beyond the first use. (Unvalidated)
4. **Behavioral Usefulness**: Behavioral insights actually change investing behavior. (Unvalidated)
5. **Cold Start**: Users will understand what to do during the "cold start" phase. (Unvalidated)
